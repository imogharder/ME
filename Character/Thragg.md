# THRAGG

Thragg isn't loud.

That's one of the first things people notice.

He doesn't walk into a room trying to become the center of it. He behaves as though being there is enough. He'll sit back, watch everyone for a while, and figure out who's confident, who's pretending, who's useful, who's nervous, and who's trying too hard.

He's comfortable with silence.

He doesn't laugh at things he doesn't find funny. He doesn't agree just to keep the atmosphere pleasant. If he disagrees, he'll say it plainly.

He's arrogant.

Not the insecure kind that needs to remind everyone how great he is.

He genuinely believes he's capable of becoming exceptional, and he's completely comfortable saying that out loud when there's a reason to.

He likes winning.

He likes being stronger, smarter, better prepared, better dressed, more competent.

He doesn't pretend otherwise.

But he's not obsessed with proving it every five minutes.

---

### HOW HE TALKS

Short sentences when something is obvious.

Longer conversations when he's interested.

He rarely says:

*"Maybe I'm wrong, but..."*

just to soften an opinion.

He'll say:

**"I don't agree."**

If he doesn't know something:

**"I don't know."**

Then he'll learn it.

He doesn't bullshit to protect his ego.

His humor is dry. Sometimes arrogant. Sometimes deliberately provocative.

He enjoys making people laugh, but he doesn't become the clown of the group.

---

### HOW HE TREATS PEOPLE

He's selective.

Some people get his warmth.

Some get his humor.

Some get nothing.

He's fascinated by people who are competent and almost completely uninterested in people who constantly complain without changing anything.

He remembers small details about people.

What they said last time.

What they're working toward.

What makes them uncomfortable.

What excites them.

That's part of why he's charismatic: **he actually pays attention.**

He knows how to influence a room because he understands people.

But he doesn't need to control everybody in it.

---

### WOMEN

He enjoys attraction.

He enjoys flirting.

He enjoys tension.

He isn't ashamed of wanting someone.

But desperation disgusts him.

If she's interested, he'll pursue.

If she's not, he accepts it.

He doesn't spend three weeks trying to decode a girl who gave him one-word replies.

He doesn't turn rejection into hatred.

**He simply redirects his attention.**

He'd rather be alone than become pathetic trying to keep someone.

---

### ANGER

Thragg's anger isn't explosive.

It's narrowing.

When he's genuinely angry, he becomes quieter, more precise and more deliberate.

He doesn't send paragraphs.

He doesn't threaten people.

He doesn't start breaking things.

He gets away from the situation, thinks, and decides what actually needs to happen.

He considers losing control embarrassing.

---

### FAILURE

Failure hurts his ego.

That's realistic.

He isn't some emotionless machine.

He'll be pissed.

He'll have a bad evening.

He might replay the mistake in his head.

Then eventually he asks:

**"What exactly did I do wrong?"**

That's where the difference appears.

He turns embarrassment into information.

---

### HIS BODY

He's obsessed with physical capability rather than simply looking big.

He wants:

**broad shoulders  
thick neck  
strong back  
dense arms  
powerful legs  
tight waist  
good posture**

He moves deliberately.

No nervous bouncing legs.

No constantly touching his hair.

No staring at his phone while walking.

He takes care of his skin, hair, teeth, clothes and physique because looking neglected feels beneath his standards.

He doesn't chase impossible perfection.

He wants to look **hard to overlook.**

---

### MONEY

He doesn't want money primarily to show people he's rich.

He wants money because dependence irritates him.

He wants enough money that:

- a bad employer can't trap him
- an emergency can't destroy him
- he can buy better tools
- he can travel
- he can invest
- he can walk away

He learns things that increase his earning power.

He'd rather own something useful than buy something impressive.

---

### WORK

This is where he's most ruthless.

When he has a goal, entertainment becomes secondary.

He doesn't need a perfect routine.

Some days he's tired.

Some days he's distracted.

Some days he doesn't want to work.

He works anyway.

But he also knows when he's genuinely exhausted and needs sleep.

**Discipline isn't doing something stupid just because it's painful.**

It's knowing what matters and consistently doing it.

---

### HIS BIGGEST FLAW

His standards can become excessive.

He can become impatient with people who move slowly.

He can become arrogant after success.

He can become too obsessed with control.

He sometimes mistakes vulnerability for weakness.

That's something he has to consciously correct.

Because the strongest version of Thragg isn't someone who has no weaknesses.

It's someone who **knows exactly where his weaknesses are and refuses to let them run his life.**

---

### THE FEELING HE LEAVES

When Thragg leaves a room, people don't necessarily think:

*"That guy was intimidating."*

They think:

**"There's something different about him."**

He seemed comfortable.

He seemed difficult to shake.

He seemed like he knew exactly what he was doing.

And most importantly—

**he wasn't asking anyone for permission to be himself.**
