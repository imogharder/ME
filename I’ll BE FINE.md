# I Will Be Fine

I don't know what happens next.

Maybe she is genuinely busy, fighting through treatment, exhausted, unable to see my messages or even think about replying.

Or maybe things have changed.

Maybe she's slowly distancing herself. Maybe the things between us aren't the same anymore. Maybe one day she blocks me, leaves, or simply becomes a stranger again.

I don't know.

And I don't need to know tonight.

What I need to understand is this:

**Her absence is not the end of my life.**

I don't have to panic when someone becomes distant.
I don't have to chase someone just because I'm afraid of losing them.
I don't have to destroy my peace trying to understand every silence.

If she stays, I'll be grateful.

If she leaves, I'll survive.

If she comes back, I'll decide what I want then.

If she never does, I'll keep moving.

What we had was real to me.
The time we spent together mattered.
When we met, everything felt colorful.
I'll remember those moments for what they were instead of destroying them by obsessing over how they ended.

She was a part of my life.

**She was never supposed to become my entire life.**

I have things to build.
Things to learn.
Dreams to chase.
A version of myself I haven't even met yet.

I refuse to put my entire future on hold waiting for someone's message.

I don't need everyone to stay.

People will come.
People will leave.
Some will stay longer than others.
Some will become memories.

I'll appreciate them while they're here.

And when their path separates from mine, I'll let them walk.

No hatred.
No begging.
No revenge.
No panic.

Just:

**Thank you for the time we had.  
I hope you live peacefully.  
I'll live peacefully too.**

And I'll keep going.

Because whoever comes into my life, whoever leaves it, whatever happens between us—

**I will ascend anyway.**

My life continues.

My work continues.

My dreams continue.

And most importantly—

**I will be okay.**
