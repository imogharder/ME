I really miss you.

I had a dream that you were texting me again, 

joking around like you always used to, 

and somehow you even brought up our future and our kids.

Waking up from that honestly hurt like hell.

I don’t know what’s happening right now, 

and I don’t want to pressure you or your family.

I just want you to know that I’m here, 


and I still care about you so damn much.

Whenever you’re able to talk again, 
I’ll be here.
