# I Miss U

It's been almost a week.

I still don't know how you're doing, and honestly, that kills me.

I keep trying to work, train, distract myself, do normal shit—but my mind keeps coming back to you.

I miss talking to you.

I miss knowing you're there.

I miss the little things.

Sometimes I just sit there thinking,

*why the hell did we even meet if it was going to become like this?*

I was miserable alone before you.

And then I finally found someone I genuinely wanted,

only for life to pull this shit.

I don't know what happens next.

So I guess all I can do is keep living my life while hoping you're okay.

I miss you.

That's it.

I just really fucking miss you.
