BETRAYAL?!

Personal Journal — October 2026
A record of what happened, what I felt, and what I still don’t know.

⸻

01. WHAT HAPPENED

On October 2, I messaged her asking if she was okay.

She replied:

“yea”
“dw”
“kuch din I wont be able to tbb”
“txt”

I understood that she wouldn’t be able to talk for a few days and that I should text her instead.

After that, I kept checking in.

* “yo”
* “r u ok??”
* “???”
* “deepali, why are u doing this”

My messages were delivered, but I didn’t receive the explanation or reassurance I needed. More than a week of silence left me confused, anxious, and wondering what had changed.

I checked her last-seen status on Truecaller and saw activity around 1:15 AM. That made me wonder whether she was available but choosing not to reply.

I started thinking she might have lost feelings or found someone else.

But I don’t have proof of that. I still don’t know the whole story.

What hurts is that I’m left trying to understand a relationship without knowing where I stand in it.

⸻

02. THE EMOTIONAL DAMAGE

I feel disappointed, betrayed, embarrassed, and emotionally exhausted.

I gave someone access to the vulnerable parts of me. I trusted her, became attached, and allowed myself to believe in what we had.

Now I’m left alone with unanswered questions.

It feels like I finally got comfortable receiving affection, only to find myself waiting for it again. The silence makes me question everything:

* Was any of it real?
* Did her feelings change?
* Did I become too attached?
* Was I not enough?
* Why couldn’t she simply explain what was happening?

I want to cry, but sometimes the tears don’t come.

My heart feels heavy. My mind keeps replaying conversations, checking message statuses, and searching for signs that might explain everything.

I hate how much power this uncertainty has over my mood, my sleep, and my peace.

I’m not just missing someone. I’m struggling with not knowing why the person I miss has become unreachable.

⸻

03. THE BETRAYAL I FEEL

The hardest part is showing someone your vulnerability and then feeling abandoned with it.

I allowed myself to care. I invested my time, attention, emotions, and trust.

I never expected a relationship to be perfect. I understood that people have difficult periods and sometimes need space.

But I wanted honesty.

If something changed, I wanted to know. If her feelings disappeared, I wanted the truth. If she needed time, I wanted to understand.

I didn’t want to become a detective, checking online activity and trying to decode silence.

I wanted communication, not clues.

I feel embarrassed that I gave someone so much of myself, only to end up questioning whether I mattered to them.

But maybe the embarrassment isn’t mine to carry.

Being sincere was never something I needed to apologize for.

⸻

04. THE ANXIETY OF ATTACHMENT

This situation has triggered something deeper in me.

I have a fear that people will show me affection, make me comfortable with their presence, and then slowly disappear once I’ve become attached.

When communication suddenly stops, I struggle to settle my mind.

I want reassurance. I want a reply. I want to know that everything is okay.

When none of that comes, my thoughts spiral.

I start checking online status, looking at delivery receipts, replaying old messages, and imagining explanations that hurt even more than the silence itself.

My anxiety and attention difficulties feel worse during these moments. I find it difficult to focus on anything else when my mind is waiting for a response.

I hate feeling like someone else’s attention determines whether I can feel okay.

But I also need to acknowledge that these feelings are real, even when the conclusions my mind reaches are uncertain.

I don’t want to keep living in this cycle.

I want to learn how to care about someone without losing myself when they become unavailable.

⸻

05. THE ANGER

I’m angry.

I’m angry that I have to sit here wondering what happened.

I’m angry that someone I trusted can leave me feeling this confused.

I’m angry that I let my guard down.

And I’m angry that this experience makes me want to stop trusting people altogether.

Part of me wants to say that I’ll never love again. That I’ll never show vulnerability again. That I’ll never allow anyone to get close enough to hurt me like this.

Part of me wants every future relationship to be different simply because I refuse to feel this powerless again.

But I know that making someone else pay for this pain would not undo what happened.

I don’t want to become the person who hurts others just because someone hurt me.

I want to become someone who recognizes red flags, establishes boundaries, and walks away when necessary.

I can become more careful without becoming cruel.

⸻

06. WHAT I KNOW VS. WHAT I FEAR

What I know

* She told me she wouldn’t be able to talk for a few days.
* I tried to check whether she was okay.
* I have not received the clarity I need.
* The silence has caused me significant emotional distress.
* I cannot force someone to communicate with me.

What I fear

* She has lost feelings for me.
* She is deliberately avoiding me.
* She has found someone else.
* I meant less to her than she meant to me.
* This will happen every time I allow myself to love someone.

These fears may feel convincing, but they are not all confirmed facts.

I refuse to confuse suspicion with certainty.

I don’t need to prove that she cheated to acknowledge that this situation is hurting me. At the same time, I owe myself honesty about what I actually know.

I can recognize the damage without pretending I know the entire story.

⸻

07. WHAT I DESERVE

I deserve honesty.

I deserve communication.

I deserve a relationship in which I don’t have to constantly investigate whether I still matter.

I deserve to express my feelings without feeling ashamed of having them.

I deserve affection that doesn’t leave me permanently anxious about when it will disappear.

That doesn’t mean someone owes me constant attention or must always be available. People have their own lives, problems, and boundaries.

But a relationship also needs mutual effort, consideration, and communication.

I can respect another person’s need for space while recognizing my own need for clarity.

I can love someone and still decide that the uncertainty has become too painful.

I don’t need to chase someone endlessly to prove that my love was genuine.

⸻

08. WHAT I NEED TO DO NOW

For my own peace, I need to stop making every unanswered message the center of my life.

I need to stop checking last-seen timestamps and treating them as evidence of my worth.

I need to stop sacrificing sleep while waiting for a notification.

I need to give myself permission to step back.

If she returns, I can listen to what she has to say and decide what is right for me.

If she continues not to respond, I can establish my own boundary instead of waiting indefinitely for an explanation.

I don’t have to send another angry message to make my pain valid.

I don’t have to prove that I’m over her.

I don’t have to make a permanent decision about love while I’m hurting.

For now, I need to take care of myself, return to my responsibilities, and give my mind some space from this situation.

⸻

09. A MESSAGE TO MY FUTURE SELF

Dear future me,

If you’re reading this months or years from now, remember how this period felt.

Remember the nights spent overthinking, the unanswered messages, the anxiety, the anger, and the disappointment.

But don’t remember it only as the time someone hurt you.

Remember it as the time you began learning the difference between loving someone and abandoning yourself for them.

You were not stupid for trusting.

You were not weak for becoming attached.

You were not embarrassing for showing someone your real emotions.

You were a person who wanted to love and be loved.

Don’t let this experience convince you that every person will betray you. Don’t punish someone new for something they never did. Don’t destroy the possibility of a healthy relationship because you are afraid of repeating an unhealthy experience.

Learn from what happened.

Pay attention to consistency, not just affection.

Look for actions that match words.

Maintain your friendships, goals, identity, and independence even when you’re deeply in love.

Learn to ask for reassurance without allowing your entire sense of safety to depend on receiving it immediately.

And when someone repeatedly leaves you confused about where you stand, remember that you are allowed to step away.

You can miss someone and still move forward.

You can love someone and still choose yourself.

You can be hurt without becoming bitter.

Most importantly, remember that this was a painful chapter of your life, not the definition of your entire future.

⸻

10. FINAL NOTE

I feel betrayed.

I feel disappointed.

I feel embarrassed that I gave someone so much access to my heart.

I feel angry that I’m left trying to understand what happened without receiving a clear answer.

I wish things had turned out differently. I wish we had communicated honestly. I wish I didn’t have to question whether the affection I received was genuine.

But I cannot force someone to give me closure.

I cannot control another person’s feelings.

I cannot change the past by repeatedly checking their online status.

And I refuse to let this situation determine the kind of person I become.

I can acknowledge that I’m hurt without allowing the hurt to become my identity.

I don’t know how this story ends yet.

What I do know is that I need to protect my peace, rebuild my stability, and stop treating another person’s silence as a measurement of my worth.

I don’t need to stop having a heart.

I need to learn how to protect it without closing it forever.

⸻

Written to get the feelings out of my head and onto the page. This is how I felt during this moment, not a permanent verdict on myself, her, or every relationship that comes after this.
